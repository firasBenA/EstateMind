# Tunisian Real Estate Contract — Mandatory Elements
# Sources: Investment Law 2016, maitre-haifaguedhami.me, spinellimechri.com

## Sale contracts (Vente immobilière)
MANDATORY: Clause suspensive — the preliminary agreement must contain a suspensive
condition making the sale contingent on obtaining the Governor's authorisation
(required for foreign buyers). Ref: Governorate permit requirement.

MANDATORY: Désignation précise du bien — full description of property including
address, surface area, land registry reference (titre foncier number).

MANDATORY: Prix et modalités de paiement — exact price in TND, payment schedule,
deposit amount (typically 10-20% refundable if Governor refuses).

MANDATORY: Droits d'enregistrement — registration fees at 3% of purchase price,
legally at charge of buyer unless otherwise agreed.

MANDATORY: Garantie d'éviction — seller guarantees buyer against third-party claims.

MANDATORY: Garantie des vices cachés — seller responsible for hidden defects.

MANDATORY: Date de transfert de propriété — exact date ownership transfers,
must coincide with registration at Conservation de la Propriété Foncière.

FORBIDDEN: Agricultural land cannot be sold to foreign buyers under any condition.
Ref: Tunisian law protecting national farmland.

REQUIRED FOR FOREIGN BUYERS: Certificate d'investissement (Fiche d'investissement)
from BCT proving funds transferred from abroad. Without this, resale proceeds
cannot be repatriated.

## Rental contracts (Bail)
MANDATORY: Durée du bail — duration must be specified. Minimum 1 year for residential.
MANDATORY: Montant du loyer — exact monthly amount in TND.
MANDATORY: Dépôt de garantie — deposit amount (typically 1-2 months rent).
MANDATORY: Indexation — whether rent can be revised and on what basis.
MANDATORY: Conditions de résiliation — notice period for termination (typically 3 months).
MANDATORY: État des lieux — inventory at entry, signed by both parties.
